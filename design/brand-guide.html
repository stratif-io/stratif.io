06 / Usage guidelines
Do
Use the logo on solid backgrounds (white, black, teal) with sufficient contrast.
Don't
Never place the logo on busy images or multicolor backgrounds without an overlay.
Do
Keep the teal accent exclusively on the "." dot and strata bars.
Don't
Don't change the teal hue or apply color to the entire text. No gradients.
Do
Maintain a clear space equal to the height of the "<" bracket around the logo.
Don't
Don't distort, skew, rotate, or add drop shadows to the logo.
